What you are paying for

The parts you never see.

A bespoke jacket is shaped long before the finished cloth is visible. The important work happens while the garment can still be changed.

I

Cut and basted

The garment begins as a shell held together in white thread. It is tried on before the seams are finished, so what does not fit can still be unpicked and corrected.

II

The basted fitting

This is the fitting that changes the pattern. Posture, shoulder balance, length and proportion can be corrected while the jacket is still half-made.

III

Canvas and construction

The chest is shaped by what sits under the cloth. Construction choices affect how the jacket moves, holds shape and behaves in Bangkok's heat and humidity.

IV

Finished and kept on file

The garment is refined on the corrected pattern, delivered to you, and your pattern stays on file for future orders and later alterations.

How it works

We come to you.

The current service is built around fittings where you already are in Bangkok — no shop journey required.

01

Tell us where & when

Share your hotel, office or home, what you want made, and your departure date if you are visiting Bangkok.

02

We bring the cloth

Cloth, bunch books and the tape come to you. Nothing is cut until you have seen the cloth in your own light.

03

Fit while half-made

The basted garment is tried on, the pattern is corrected on your body, and later fittings refine the cloth against that pattern.

04

Delivered to you

The finished garment comes back to you, and your pattern is kept on file so later orders can begin from the original draft.
